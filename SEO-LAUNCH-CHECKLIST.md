# Sheffield SEO launch checklist

The website now includes search-focused titles and descriptions, local business structured data, a crawlable sitemap, robots instructions, canonical URLs, internal service links and dedicated pages for each core service.

Website SEO is only one part of strong local rankings. Complete these actions after the custom domain is connected.

## 1. Google Search Console

1. Add and verify `amexectravel.co.uk` in Google Search Console.
2. Submit `https://www.amexectravel.co.uk/sitemap.xml`.
3. Inspect the home page and each service page, then request indexing.
4. Check indexing and search performance monthly.

## 2. Google Business Profile

1. Claim and verify AM Executive Travel & Transfers Ltd.
2. Use the same business name, phone number, website and Sheffield service area shown on the website.
3. Select the closest accurate primary business category and add each genuine service.
4. Add opening/contact hours and the new professional photographs when ready.
5. Request genuine Google reviews after completed journeys and reply to every review.

Google states that local results are mainly based on relevance, distance and prominence. Complete information, reviews, photographs and links from other reputable websites all contribute.

## 3. Local authority and trust

- Keep business details consistent across Companies House, social profiles and reputable business directories.
- Seek genuine links and mentions from partner organisations, venues, hotels, Sheffield business groups and clubs where appropriate.
- Publish real client feedback only after permission is obtained.
- Add useful, original website content about journeys and services rather than repeating keywords.

## 4. After the custom domain goes live

- Confirm every page loads securely with HTTPS.
- Confirm the GitHub staging address redirects or points search engines to the custom domain.
- Test the sitemap and robots file in Search Console.
- Monitor enquiries and the search terms that generate them.

No company can guarantee a number-one Google position. Consistent reviews, accurate local information, authority and a technically sound website create the strongest legitimate foundation.
